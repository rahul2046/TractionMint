FAQ

Common Questions

How long until we see inbound leads?

While impressions and follower counts increase in the first 14 days, converting that attention into pipeline usually takes 45 to 60 days of consistent, high-quality execution.

Do you write the posts for us?

Yes. We operate as an executive ghostwriting service. We interview you, extract your insights, and our editorial team drafts the content perfectly matching your voice.

Will this look like an agency is running my account?

No. Our onboarding process involves deep tone-of-voice analysis. Your content will sound exactly like you on your most articulate day, not like generic marketing copy.

Are there long-term contracts?

We work on a rolling 90-day cycle, as this is the minimum time frame required to build a compounding algorithm system. After that, we operate month-to-month.
